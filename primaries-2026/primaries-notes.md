# Texas 2026 Primaries: Notes from the Early-Vote Analysis

*Compiled Oct. 30, 2026, from primary-related material gathered while analyzing the general-election early vote. This is not a full primaries archive. Add primary results files here as they come in.*

## March 3, 2026 primary turnout

Texas publishes no single statewide primary turnout figure, so outlets count votes in a benchmark race. That's why the totals below differ.

| Source | Democratic | Republican |
|---|---|---|
| KERA | ~2.3M | ~2.16M (4.4M+ combined) |
| KXAN tracker | ~2.1M | ~1.96M |
| Texas Tribune-style tally (via The Texan) | ~2.3M | ~2.2M |
| Early voting only (tracker cited in search) | 1,495,922 | 1,345,735 (D +150,187) |

- Democrats out-voted Republicans, only the second time in recent cycles (after 2020).
- Reported as the highest early-voting turnout ever for a Texas midterm primary (thinly sourced).
- Transparency USA Democratic primary tracker: 1,435,111 early voters, Feb. 17–27; peak day Feb. 27 (307,333).
- May runoff (Democratic): 305,678 early voters, May 18–22 (Transparency USA).

Sources: [KERA](https://www.keranews.org/texas-news/2026-03-06/texas-election-turnout-voting-democrat-republican), [The Texan](https://thetexan.news/elections/2026/nearly-4-5-million-texans-participated-in-march-primaries-democratic-voters-led-with-turnout/article_5e167552-d8ec-45e5-a776-6a83e51c50cc.html), [KXAN](https://www.kxan.com/news/your-local-election-hq/march-2026-turnout-tracker/), [Transparency USA, Dem primary](https://turnout.transparencyusa.org/2026-primary-dem/), [Transparency USA, Dem runoff](https://turnout.transparencyusa.org/2026-primary-runoff-dem/)

| 2026 | ~2.1–2.3M | ~1.96–2.2M | See above |

**Why it matters for the general:** voter-history firms classify people by which primaries they've voted in. Before 2018, Democratic primaries were small, so many Democrats were filed as "general-only" voters. The 2020 and 2026 surges moved many of them into the Democratic-primary column. See [`../general-2026/early-vote/analysis.md`](../general-2026/early-vote/analysis.md), "Historical lean of non-primary voters."
